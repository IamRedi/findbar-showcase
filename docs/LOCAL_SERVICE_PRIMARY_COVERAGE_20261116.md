## Behavior and boundaries

New business audits can add six bounded, low-severity manual reviews: a generic service title despite a specific primary heading; a contact page whose description does not describe contact; substantial service copy repeated under different headings; the explanation of a quotation process; concrete project references; and optional entity structured data. The first three are editorial review candidates. The last three are opportunities requiring confirmation, not proof of missing prices, experience or identity. No draft text, automatic repair, publication or business assertion is generated.

Version1 `bounded_local_service_signals_v1` records primary-content and structural evidence. `bounded_local_service_review_v1` supplies per-page decisions, reasons and source hashes. The finite DE/EN/IT/SQ vocabulary needs corroborated local-service context and a business entity. Software products, intentional demos/noindex, transport errors, unreadable shells, non-offer page roles, unsupported language, editorial/legal/job/tutorial/directory contexts and insufficient primary text abstain. Hidden, quoted, template, footer and carousel material cannot justify a service-copy warning. Declared website names remain observations, not verified legal identity or an alias conflict.

Quote review recognizes project details both in visible prose and usable form labels, including measurements, site/access, photographs and a handling step such as preparing or clarifying an offer. All bounded forms are considered; an earlier plain form cannot hide a later detailed form. Hidden form labels and CSS-hidden success/failure messages do not count as a completed request or an error. No form is submitted and entered field values are never read. A complete quotation explanation suppresses this suggestion; a public fixed price is never required. Concrete dated projects/captions can support references, but visual material or information elsewhere remains possible.

Entity-schema review is optional and homepage-scoped. JSON-LD graphs, microdata and RDFa are considered. Malformed, scalar, unfamiliar or over-budget structures remain unverified rather than confidently absent. New review excerpts omit HTTP(S) URL values; the existing URL-only-description rule retains its own sanitized evidence and does not get a duplicate contact-description review.

Reading is bounded to24,000 primary characters,160 structural blocks,16 forms,40 labels/form and500 schema nodes/depth8. Truncation suppresses unsupported quotation/reference absence suggestions. The service vocabulary is deliberately finite; this does not cover all businesses, languages or semantic defects.
